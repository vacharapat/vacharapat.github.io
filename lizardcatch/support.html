How to play

Tap Play, then hold your phone up and turn your body to look around the room. When a lizard is inside the ring in the middle of the screen, snap it:

  • Foldable iPhone: fold the phone shut. The cover screen shows what's in your trap. Open the phone fully to keep playing. The round timer stops while the phone is folded.
  • Other iPhones: tap the screen.

The ring turns orange when a lizard is inside and purple when an insect is. Snapping a butterfly or dragonfly costs points. Each round lasts 60 seconds.

Do I need to fold fast or hard?

No. A gentle, normal fold works exactly the same as a fast one. Only your timing counts: what's in the ring when you start folding.

The room drifts or isn't centered

Tap the target button at the top right to recenter the view in front of you.

Items

Bait draws every lizard on the wall toward the ring. A clock adds time to the round. You can use each kind once per round, and you get one free bait every day.

Purchases

Restore purchases

Open the Shop from the start or results screen and tap Restore Purchases. This restores Remove Ads, scenes, and packs on any device signed in with the same Apple Account.

Bait and clocks

Bait and clocks are used up as you play, so the App Store can't restore them. They are saved on your device and included in your device backups, but deleting the app deletes them.

Refunds

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads and privacy

Ads appear only between rounds, never during a round. Rewarded ads are always optional. Remove Ads stops all ads. To change your ad consent, open the Shop and tap Ad Privacy Choices (shown in regions where it's required). Read the Privacy Policy.
