Lizard Catch

Fold your phone to snap! · พับจอ งับจิ้งจก!

Lizards are running all over the walls of your room. Turn around to scan the room, and when a lizard runs into the ring, fold your phone shut to snap it. On an iPhone that doesn't fold, just tap the screen.
